The studio

One founder, an AI team, and taste.

Rahul Sakharkar started Gully Brands in 2026 after five years at InsuranceMarket.ae in Dubai, where he was promoted through four roles, from policy operations to running the training department. He came home to Mumbai to make the kind of work small businesses are usually priced out of.

The studio is one person plus a set of AI agents he built himself. They do the heavy lifting: research, first drafts, image and video generation on the studio’s own GPUs, and the testing before anything goes live. He directs, decides, writes and shoots. That is how a one-person studio ships websites, films and campaigns in weeks, at prices a neighbourhood shop can afford.

Everything here was made with that setup, from the Ishq film to a menu tool a food stall uses every night.
