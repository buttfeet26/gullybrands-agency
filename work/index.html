AI influencer model · for Gully Brands Clothing

Meet Mia.

Mia is an AI influencer model I made for work. She wears every crop top in the Gully Brands store: one studio shot per colour and one lifestyle shot per design, 54 photos across 12 designs. The print on her top is the real product, placed exactly.

  1. Train her identityA custom LoRA on Krea 2, built from a curated dataset, so she stays the same person in a café, on a beach or in a studio.
  2. Pose the shotKrea 2 + the Mia LoRA set the scene, lighting and pose per design's vibe.
  3. Dress her for realQwen-Image edit puts the exact product photo on her, so print, colour and placement match what ships.
  4. QC every frameSpelling and placement of the print, colour, face in frame, nothing covering the graphic.
