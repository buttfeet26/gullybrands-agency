Since 1984

The first surgicalshop in Ulhasnagar.Still here.

Mamta Surgicals opened opposite the Shiv Mandir on Baba Dholuram Darbar Road when there was nowhere else in town to buy a wheelchair. Two generations later, the counter is the same, the range is ten times bigger, and most customers were sent by someone we helped before.

We don't sell what you don't need. If a rental makes more sense than buying, we'll say so.
