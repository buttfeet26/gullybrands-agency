## 3. What they DON'T have

- **No website.** mamtasurgical.com / .in, mamtasurgicals.com / .in all unresolved → available to register.
- **No WhatsApp Business catalogue / click-to-chat** anywhere public. Justdial routes enquiries through its own WhatsApp.
- **No rental page.** Rentals (beds, wheelchairs, likely oxygen concentrators) are their highest-intent product and appear only as a Justdial tag.
- **No reels, no video, no product photos on Google** beyond what JD scraped. Nothing on YouTube.
- **No reviews in >1 year** on Google; no review-ask flow; no owner replies.
- **No ads.** Nothing in Meta Ad Library or Google Ads surfaced.
- **No marketplace** presence (Amazon/Flipkart/Meesho).
- **Name hygiene:** "Mamta Surgical" vs "Mamta Surgicals", 3 PIN codes, 4 phone numbers, Sunday hours wrong on Mappls, duplicate Justdial listing. NAP (name-address-phone) consistency is broken, which hurts Maps ranking.

## 6. Customer language (VoC seeds for copy)

From Google/Justdial/Worldorgs reviews: *"value for money", "genuine product at reasonable price", "owner guided us perfectly", "very polite", "full variety", "demonstration of tech product", "everything from spirometers to wheelchairs", "100% will recommend for all your healthcare requirements", "first time at this shop… satisfied"*.
Themes: trust + guidance (owner explains the product), range, price. Not a single review mentions rentals, delivery or hours — that's the untold story.
